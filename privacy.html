Privacy Policy (गोपनीयता नीति)

Apna eMitra (apnaemitra.in) par aapki privacy aur data security hamari sabse badi prathmikta hai. Yah Privacy Policy batati hai ki jab aap hamari website ya mobile web app (PWA) ka upyog karte hain, to hum kaun si jankari ekatra (collect) karte hain aur uska upyog kaise karte hain.

1. Hum Kaun Si Jankari Collect Karte Hain?

  • Online Form Assistance ke dauran: Jab aap kisi sarkari ya private job ka form bharwane ke liye request bhejte hain, to hum aapka Naam, 10-digit WhatsApp Mobile Number, Form ka naam, Payment UTR/Transaction ID, aur aapke dwara upload kiye gaye documents (PDF/Photo) collect karte hain.
  • Status Tracking: Aapke dwara darj kiya gaya mobile number keval aapke aavedan ka status dikhane aur WhatsApp par update bhejne ke liye upyog hota hai.
  • Technical Data: Website ki performance behtar banane ke liye basic browser aur device information (cookies/cache) ka upyog ho sakta hai.

2. Aapke Data aur Documents ka Upyog Kaise Hota Hai?

  • Aapke documents ka upyog keval aapke dwara mange gaye online form ko bharne ke liye hi kiya jata hai.
  • Hum aapka personal data ya documents kisi bhi third-party marketing company ko bechte ya share nahi karte.
  • Aapka data surakshit cloud database (Supabase) par encrypted roop mein store rehta hai, jise keval adhikrit (authorized) admin hi access kar sakta hai.

3. Third-Party Links (Sarkari Portals)

Hamari website par sarkari naukriyon, admit card aur yojanaon ke adhikarik links (jaise ssc.gov.in, upsc.gov.in, rrbcdg.gov.in, rssb.rajasthan.gov.in aadi) diye jate hain. Un external websites par jane ke baad unki apni Privacy Policy lagu hoti hai.

Terms & Conditions (नियम एवं शर्तें)

apnaemitra.in ka upyog karke aap niche di gayi sharton se sahmat hote hain:

  • Sahi Jankari Dena: Online form assistance lete samay sahi documents aur clear photo/PDF upload karna aavedak (user) ki zimmedari hai. Galat document ke karan form reject hone par Apna eMitra zimmedar nahi hoga.
  • Seva Shulk (Service Charge): Hamare dwara online form bharne ki sahayata ke liye nirdharit sarkari/portal fees ke atirikt ₹50 (ya nirdharit) seva shulk liya jata hai. Form submit hone ke baad service charge refundable nahi hoga.
  • Final Verification: Form submit hone ke baad prapt receipt/acknowledgement slip ko dhyan se check karna student ki zimmedari hogi.
  • Niyamon mein Badlav: Apna eMitra ko bina kisi purv suchna ke website ki samagri ya seva shulkon mein badlav karne ka adhikar surakshit hai.
